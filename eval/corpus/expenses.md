# Travel and expenses

This policy covers business travel, meals and equipment. When in doubt, spend company money as if
it were your own.

## Travel

Book travel through the company travel portal. Economy class is the default for flights under six
hours; business class may be booked for longer flights with the approval of a director. Train
travel is preferred over flying for journeys that take less than four hours by train. Hotels are
reimbursed up to 180 euros per night, or up to 250 euros per night in London, Paris and Zurich.

## Meals

While travelling, meals are reimbursed up to 60 euros per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at
customer dinners approved in advance by a manager.

## Home office

Every employee receives a home office budget of 500 euros per year for furniture and equipment such
as a desk, chair or monitor. The budget does not roll over into the next year.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense, with a receipt for every item over 25 euros.
Claims above 500 euros need approval from your manager before they are reimbursed. Approved claims
are paid out with the next monthly salary.
