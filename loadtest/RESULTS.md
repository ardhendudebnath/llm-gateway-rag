## Chaos test

A steady 20 req/s to the `mock` route with the cache off. At 15s the primary deployment was made to fail every call (through the admin fault-injection API, on every replica); at 60s the fault was cleared.

| Phase | Requests | Succeeded | Served by primary | p50 | p95 | Cost / 1k requests |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| healthy | 300 | 100.0% | 100.0% | 83 ms | 84 ms | $0.027 |
| fault | 900 | 100.0% | 1.3% | 153 ms | 155 ms | $0.13356 |
| recovery | 900 | 100.0% | 64.4% | 84 ms | 154 ms | $0.0654 |

- **User-visible failures: 0.** Every request during the fault was answered, by the fallback.
- **Breakers opened 0.9 s after the fault**, after 7 failed primary calls, all absorbed by fallback: 3 per replica (the breaker threshold), plus one already in flight. The first failure lands up to ~1 s after injection because each replica caches the fault table for a second.
- 2 failed half-open probes while the primary stayed down: one per replica per 30 s cooldown, checking whether it had recovered.
- **Primary serving again 15.95 s after the fix**: the breakers re-check once per cooldown, so recovery takes up to 30 s after a fix.
- Failed primary calls, seconds after the fault: [0.6, 0.65, 0.7, 0.75, 0.8, 0.85, 0.9, 30.9, 31.0]
